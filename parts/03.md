**Q53. [Single]** A 9-year-old on griseofulvin for tinea capitis comes back with a tender, boggy, pustular mass on the scalp and severe inflammation. Which plan is most appropriate?

B. Stop griseofulvin and apply ketoconazole 2% cream to the mass twice daily
C. Continue griseofulvin and add oral prednisone tapered over 2–3 weeks
D. Continue griseofulvin and add oral prednisone for 5–14 days; consider a systemic antibiotic if secondary bacterial infection is suspected

**Answer:** D
**Rationale:** This is a kerion. Per the professor, a kerion with severe inflammation is treated with prednisone for 5–14 days while oral antifungal therapy continues, with systemic antibiotics considered for secondary bacterial infection.
